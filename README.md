# Jordan Lieberman

CEO of [Powers Interactive](https://powersinteractive.com), a non-partisan political digital media firm that plans and buys programmatic advertising for candidates, causes, and brands.

I write [VendorTrap](https://vendortrap.substack.com), a newsletter on the business of political advertising. My essays are collected at [jslieberman-writing.github.io](https://jslieberman-writing.github.io).

Before Powers, I was publisher of Campaigns & Elections and held senior roles at Audience Partners, a4, and FourthWall. AAPC 40 Under 40 (2015). Based in South Carolina.
